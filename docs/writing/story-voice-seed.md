## Voice checklist

- Address the player intimately, usually in second person.
- Start with absurdly sincere, overdramatic stakes or sensory detail; land on a mundane baseball, school, snack, or bureaucracy joke.
- Keep popup copy phone-readable: compact title and roughly two to five sentences.
- Escalate from backyard baseball to cosmic stakes without abandoning baseball as the literal solution.
- Let humor come from the narrator taking a ridiculous premise completely seriously.
- Preserve authored boss signatures and concrete canonical facts; do not introduce lore that changes mechanics.

## Canonical facts

- Little Timmy is the opening opponent; the Wiffle ball travels two feet, rolls to his feet, and reveals the need for practice.
- Genetic rebirth returns the player to toddlerhood with retained prestige upgrades and conspicuous modifications; Little Timmy is the first imagined casualty of a conquest of the stars.
- The first return to Middle School after genetic rebirth gets its own once-only Axe-body-spray/acne beat. It does not occur in the first lifetime.
- Coach Pitch remains a recognizable league label, but the player throws and defends the mound; adults merely organize the battlefield.
- Octathulhu has eight bats and will eat the universe unless beaten at baseball.
